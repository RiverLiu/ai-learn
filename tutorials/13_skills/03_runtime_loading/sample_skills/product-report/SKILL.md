# Product Report

Workflow:

1. Identify the audience and reporting period.
2. Separate facts, metric interpretation, risks, and recommendations.
3. Highlight metric movement, likely causes, and uncertainty.
4. Use concise executive language.
5. Do not hide missing or unreliable data.
